# Service integrations in OpenTune builds

The app talks to YouTube Music, your own music server, and the public
endpoints behind lyrics and metadata. Nothing else.

Scrobbling to Last.fm or ListenBrainz is not part of this build. A song's
listening history stays on the device and, if you sign in, goes to your
YouTube Music account. Your own music server still gets the scrobbles it
needs to record plays.
